JERZEE HERO VIDEO INSTRUCTIONS
===============================

To display your own background video in the hero section:
1. Place your MP4 video file inside this folder (`/public/videos/`).
2. Name the file `hero.mp4` (so the path is `/public/videos/hero.mp4`).
3. Reload the website. The hero section will automatically detect and play your video in the background!

Video Specifications:
- Format: MP4 (H.264 video codec recommended for universal browser support)
- Attributes applied: autoplay, muted, loop, playsinline
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 widescreen recommended
- If no video is present, the website automatically displays the high-resolution fallback image (`/images/hero_fallback.jpg`).
