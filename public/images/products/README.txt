JERZEE PRODUCT IMAGES INSTRUCTIONS
==================================

Existing bundled product images are kept in this folder. To add or update a product:
1. Sign in to the admin portal and open the Products tab.
2. Upload a PNG, JPEG, or WebP image (up to 650 KB), or provide an HTTPS image URL.
3. To use a bundled image, place it in this folder and enter its `/images/products/<filename>` path.
4. Existing bundled filenames include `acmilan.jpg`, `bangladeshhome.jpg`, `barcalona.jpg`,
   `ferrari.jpg`, `realmadrid.jpg`, and `redbull.jpg`.
5. Recommendation: square (1:1) aspect ratio on a dark or isolated background (e.g. 800x800 or 1000x1000).
