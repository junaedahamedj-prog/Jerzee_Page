JERZEE PRODUCT IMAGES INSTRUCTIONS
==================================

To use your own product jersey images:
1. Place your image files inside this folder (`/public/images/products/`).
2. Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp, .svg.
3. Default filenames in the catalog:
   - barcelona.jpg (or barcelona.png/webp)
   - real-madrid.jpg (or real-madrid.png/webp)
   - bangladesh-cricket.jpg (or bangladesh-cricket.png/webp)
   - red-bull-f1.jpg (or red-bull-f1.png/webp)
   - ferrari-f1.svg (or ferrari-f1.jpg/png)
   - bangladesh-football.svg (or bangladesh-football.jpg/png)
4. To add new products or change filenames, simply edit `/data/products.json`.
5. Recommendation: Square (1:1) aspect ratio on a dark/isolated background (e.g. 800x800 or 1000x1000) for the most premium sportswear look.
